Financial, mental, physical. The more you work on your health and your head, the easier business gets. The less you do, the harder every client, every pitch and every price feels.

Creative skill alone doesn't win any more. There's too much noise. You need the conviction to take yourself to market and the expertise that makes clients come to you. You don't show up like that burnt out.

Freedom is choice. Doing what you want, with the people you love, when you want.

04 / The Locked In Ritual

Four habits. Every day.

  1. 01
    Feed the mind. Books, wisdom, people who lift you.
  2. 02
    Calm the body. Walks, breathing, a page of journaling.
  3. 03
    Move the body. Level one is three times a week. Level two is five. Level three is every day.
  4. 04
    Fuel the body. Protein, real food. The trainer agent builds your plan if you want one.

Ticked off in the portal every day. Your Core Four is scored on day one and day ninety. This isn't a body transformation programme. It's how you get your power back.
