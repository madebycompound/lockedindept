03 / Who's behind it

The bigger the climb in business, the bigger the climb you make inside your own head.

I've been on the entrepreneur path since I was 13: twenty-two years of building, more than a decade behind a camera, retainers from £1,000 to $20,000 a month. Through my agency I worked as creative and brand strategist with clients doing £200k+ months. I've taken creatives from burning out at £5k months to their first £20k, £25k and £30k months. The work was never what held them back. It was what they believed they were allowed to charge, and whether they backed themselves. I find where you're holding yourself back, and I don't let you stay there.
