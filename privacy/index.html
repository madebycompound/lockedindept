Why we use it

To send your score and follow-up emails you asked for, to assess your application, to deliver the programme, and to keep in touch about it. Our legal basis is your consent (for the score and emails) and the contract between us (for members). You can unsubscribe from any email with one click.

Who we share it with

Only the services that run the site for us: Vercel (hosting), Supabase (database), Resend (email) and Anthropic (powers the members' AI assistant). Some of these store data outside the UK, under standard data protection safeguards. We never sell your data or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Score results and enquiries: up to two years, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Member records: for as long as you're a member, then up to six years for tax and accounting.
